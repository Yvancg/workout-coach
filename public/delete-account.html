Delete your Workout Coach account

Workout Coach provides self-service permanent account deletion. You do not need to reinstall the Android app to request deletion.

Open the secure deletion flow below, sign in to the account you want to delete, and confirm deletion. The process removes the account's synced workout logs and session history, account-linked security records, and Supabase authentication account.
