Workout Coach Privacy Policy

Last updated: 9 October 2026

Workout Coach is a workout planning and logging application operated by Y Consulting LLC. This policy explains what information the app handles, why it is used, where it is stored, and how you can delete it.

Information the app handles

The app can store workout selections, exercise and set logs, session history, session notes, available dumbbell weights, training readiness, completed repetitions or time, actual load, and optional RPE. It also stores app preferences needed to run a workout session.

If you sign in, Supabase Auth processes your login identity, which may include your email address and information supplied by Google when you choose Google sign-in. Workout Coach does not request access to Google Fit or Health Connect.

Local-only use

You can use Workout Coach without signing in. In local-only mode, workout state and history are stored on your device. They are not synced to the Workout Coach cloud service.

Signed-in sync

If you sign in, Workout Coach uses Supabase for authentication and Cloudflare Workers and D1 for private workout synchronization. Your authenticated user identifier and email address are used to keep your records separated from other users.

How information is used

Information is used to provide workout guidance, preserve session progress, display workout history, synchronize your history across signed-in devices, and produce transparent progression suggestions based on your prior workout records.

Workout Coach does not sell personal information and does not use workout or authentication information for advertising.

Service providers

Supabase provides authentication services. Cloudflare provides application hosting, API execution, security controls, and the D1 database used for signed-in workout synchronization. Google provides the Google sign-in service when you choose that option.

Security and technical logs

The service uses security controls such as authenticated API requests, origin restrictions, rate limiting, and security audit events. Account-linked audit records are deleted as part of self-service account deletion. Technical security records that are not directly linked to an account may be retained when necessary to protect the service from abuse and investigate security incidents.

Data retention and deletion

Local workout information remains on your device until you clear it, remove the app's data, or complete account deletion from that device. Signed-in workout information remains in the synchronization service until you delete individual sessions or delete your account.

You can permanently delete your account and associated synced workout data through the in-app account controls or the public account deletion page. The deletion flow removes the account's synced workout logs and session history, account-linked security records, and the Supabase authentication account. It also clears local workout data on the device used to complete deletion.

Health information

Workout Coach handles fitness and activity information that you choose to enter or generate while using the app. It is intended for workout organization and coaching support, not medical diagnosis or treatment.

Your choices

You may use the app without an account, export local workout logs as CSV, delete individual synced sessions, clear local data, sign out, or permanently delete your account and synced data.

Contact

Privacy questions can be sent to contact@y-consulting.us.
